# Product Marketing Playbook — AdsCommand

**Version:** 1.0-local  
**Decision role:** context only; never chooses budget or provider action.

## Required business context

- Company: MBC/MBI
- Product/service and product group
- Target audience
- USP and approved claims
- Forbidden claims/words
- Target CPA/CPL/ROAS
- Minimum margin target (BA-approved)
- Protected campaigns and approval owner

## Checklist

- [ ] Product profile has owner and updatedAt.
- [ ] Claims are approved by BA/owner.
- [ ] Targets have source, period and currency.
- [ ] Protected campaigns are listed.
- [ ] Policy version is recorded in every recommendation.

## Guardrail

This playbook may explain or validate campaign context. It must not call Meta/Google mutation clients or set a proposed budget.
